# Readings (4 October 2026)

Three kinds of words, kept apart:
- CAPITALS = spelled with the symbol alphabet (read sign by sign).
- plain = a figure with a period gloss for this figure and list (`lists_v4.tsv`).
- {braces} = a window proposal: no gloss; the word is chosen inside the alphabetical window. A proposal only.
- [..] = no value. (?) = doubt. "quotes" = clear text of the letter (my reading of the hand).

The work was done by a model. No palaeographer has checked it.

---

## 1. BnF fr. 4696 f. 113r — Scipione Gonzaga to Nevers, Rome, end of September 1589. NO period gloss.

Two passes (coordinator; blind agent): same figure 98 %, same mark 89 % (64 figures). Transcription:
`tr/f113r_consensus.txt`.

> "... di dirle, che" poco gusto HANNO DATO a Papa la lettera di [Duca di] Nevers(?) che Volta troppo per(?)
> ANDAVA SPARGENDO per la {corte}, di modo che per questo causa(?) il rispetto di V.E. non GLI PUO(?) essere
> di tanto {giovamento} quanto SARIA stato in altro tempo. "L'aggiunse" a [97] che essendo stato INTERCETTE
> [18] mie lettere [21] quello di Volta, io non POSSO RISCALDARMI SOPRA CIO(?) per lui, che non MOSTRI essere
> CONSAPEVOLE et troppo parte in CIO che SCRIVEVA il Volta; ne però LASCIO di FARE quanto bene(?) POSSA.
> "il che tutto sia per aviso a V. Ecc."

English: "... to tell you that the letters of the Duke of Nevers, which Volta spread too freely about the
court, gave the Pope little pleasure, so that for this reason the regard for Your Excellency cannot help him
as much as it would have at another time. He added that, since my letters were intercepted [with] that of
Volta, I cannot be warm in this matter for him, lest I show that I know and take too much part in what Volta
wrote; yet I do not fail to do what good I can."

Notes: 69 is a plain figure (names are plain figures with a point in fr. 4696: 69 = Nevers on ff. 30r, 44v).
{corte} = C 61, between corrivo 59 and cosa 63. {giovamento} = G 27, between gagliardo 14 and giudicio 30.
"causa" = C 14 (gloss of f. 101r). "essendo" = (28) (gloss of f. 46v). Not resolved: 97 with a dot (a word of
list A between capo 95 and casa 98, or a name), 18 with two bars (between "almeno" 17 and "ancora" 19), 21.
Control: the clear text of the same weeks says the same (f. 104r, 9 September 1589: Volta was taken to prison;
the Cardinal does not know if the letters "siano state intercette a Lione": survey agent's reading at 900 px).

## 2. BnF fr. 4696 ff. 62r-v, f. 65r — March and April 1587. NO period gloss. Symbol alphabet only.

Two blind passes: 378 of 379 letters the same (99.7 %). Files `tr/unglossed1587_passA.tsv`, `_passB.tsv`.
Clear words in lower case are the agents' reading of the hand (not checked by me).

f. 62r: "gli ho ricordato il negotio di" CAVALLI ... "quanto alla" PRESTANZA(?) NON "crede che potria esser"
MINORE DI CINQANTA SCUDI PER CAVALLO, "ma ben potrebbe esser" MAGIORE, "caso che in un medesimo" TEMPO "si
facessero altre" SPEDITIONI, "per la" CARESTIA DE CAVALLI. ... "consideratione al" CAMINO CHE DOVRANO FAR
QESTE GENTI "per haverlo" LIBERO ... "da" GUARDAR DI PIGLIAR GENTE "per causa di gl'" INTERESSI DE Principe
(figure 41 with a bar; "Principe" by lists_v3).

f. 62v: "intorno al" NUMERO DE CAVALLI "per ciascuna" COMPAGNIA ... "il" NUMERO DE [49: "cap." on f. 46v]
... LE GUIDE et COMPAGNIA PRATICA "appresso i" FORIERI ... "versato nel" MESTIERO DE LA GUERRA. "Quanto poi a"
LA PERSONA SUA EGLI SI OFERISCE PRONTISIMO A SERVIRLA ... "in caso di" DIFESA O VERO DI ESALTATIONE DI LEI ...
RIGUARDO A L INTERESSE "che tiene" LA CASA CON Re di Spagna (47 with a bar).

f. 65r: "... conforme all'ordine suo" POSTI NELLI FOGLI DI SABATO ... "con ogni" DISIMULATIONE, "essendo così
piaciuto a" Volta (70 with a bar).

English: a soldier (not named in the cipher) says that the advance for raising horse cannot be less than
fifty scudi a horse, and more if other levies are made at the same time, because horses are scarce; the road
that the troops must take needs thought; he offers his own person "most readily" to serve the Duke, above all
for his defence or his rise, with regard to the interest that his house has with the King of Spain.

## 3. BnF fr. 4696 f. 42v, f. 43r — 3 November 1586. NO period gloss.

Two passes by agents (`tr/unglossed1586_passA.tsv`, `_passB.tsv`); they differ on several marks.

f. 42v: "vidi però alcuni pochi dì sono" [42, a person], "il quale mi accennò così in passando, che" con
difficile(?) si [31] la(?) [45] "ne io per esser in presenza di molti gli risposi altro se non che era"
MEGLIO non FARE che FAR(?) male. ... la PROMOTIONE oltre NATALE con {speranza}(?) che io VI POSSA essere
COMPRESA(?), fine(?) suo et di [45].
f. 43r: "dal" Signore VESPASIANO GONZAGA [a] Re di Spagna.

English (weak): a person told him in passing that [something] would be hard ...; he answered only that it was
better not to do than to do badly. The promotion [of cardinals] is put off beyond Christmas, with a hope that
he can be in it. {speranza} = S 80, between sospetto 73 and spirito 83.

## 4. BnF fr. 4698 f. 108r — the Duke of Nevers to Cardinal Scipione Gonzaga, Decize, 28 February 1590 (copy). NO period decipherment found.

Passes: lines 1-2 two passes (figure 92 %, mark 74 %); line 1 a third look; lines 3-5 ONE blind pass.
This is a first reading with our rebuilt key. It is weak: read it as a word list, not as a text.
Transcription: `tr/n108r_consensus.txt`. Almost all units are figures.

> "hoggi solamente" ho scritto [78: Duca di Mantova, if a name; "desiderio" if list C] da detto(?) sopra
> detto(?) in tale {modo} Scipione, ma scritto che a(?) la havuto parola a fine(?) che [34 with a bar: a person]
> servitio a Re di Spagna, et che come rispondere desiderio sapere in quale {modo} ho in A H, et come il che ho
> fatto per havere tempo(?) di sapere la volontà di S.A., poi che così la [13] che la [95] nostro si [..]
> necessità. "et per ciò la prego di avisarmi del suo" interesse(?) "perche certo [ha] da considerar assai.
> Donque" loro(?) "havrà buon campo longo di poter" havere scritto(?) Z "come desidero che ..."

English (the sense that is safe): Nevers says that only today he wrote [to the Duke of Mantua?]; that the
matter is the service of the King of Spain; that, before he answers, he wants to know "in which way" he is to
be in "A" and "H" (two code letters); that he did this to have [time] to learn the will of His Highness (the
Duke of Mantua); and he asks the Cardinal to tell him his view, "because there is much to consider".
Control: this fits the clear minute of 31 January 1590 (fr. 4698 ff. 97r-98r, earlier project): "desidero essere
chiaro in qual modo intende l'Amb.re di Spagna che debba servire il Re Catt.co".
{modo} = M 61 or 62 (the two passes differ), between mio 54 and molto 65. "tempo" = T 26, if the mark is a
bar above and a dot below; as read (bar above only) it is N 26 "legato".

## 5. BnF fr. 4698 f. 179r — the Duke of Nevers to Cardinal Scipione Gonzaga, Nevers, 9 July 1590. NO period decipherment found.

The letter is clear Italian with eight figures and one spelled word. Two passes (coordinator; blind agent):
same figure 7 of 8 (13 against 23; the image shows 13); same mark 6 of 7.

> "... che confusion[e], poi che" Papa "va indugiando per non" [87: volontà/volere(?) if list T; staffetta if
> list S] danaro, "perche cosa certa è che" Navarra "non attende di" essere Catolici [= cattolico], "che così
> la referì" [13 with a bar: a person] "a" [69: Duca di Nevers, if a name] "onde il proverbio di" NERONE
> "havrà loco".

English: "... confusion, since the Pope delays so as not to [give] money; for it is certain that Navarre does
not mean to become a Catholic, as [a person] reported to [Nevers?]; so the proverb of Nero will apply."
On the same page Nevers explains a way to tell figures apart by "ponti" above and below (38 "col ponto sopra il
otto", 97 "colli ponti di sotto") and speaks of "uno novo alfabeto" that he sent.
