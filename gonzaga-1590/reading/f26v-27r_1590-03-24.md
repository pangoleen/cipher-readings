# Scipione Gonzaga to Nevers, Rome, 24 March 1590 — BnF fr. 4698 ff. 26v-27r — reading, version 4

Status: 4 October 2026. Made by a model. No palaeographer has checked it. No period decipherment of this letter was
found. This version replaces `gonzaga-1590/reading/f26v-27r_1590-03-24.md` (version 3).

## What changed since version 3

- The table is `lists_v4.tsv` (448 values). Eight proposals of version 3 now have a period gloss (fr. 4696).
- I looked at every cipher line on enlarged crops of the film (`v4/lc_26v_L*.jpg`, `v4/lc_27r_R*.jpg`, zooms
  `v4/z_*.jpg`). This look is NOT blind: I knew the table. The two blind passes of version 3 stay the base.
- 15 marks and 7 figures are read in a new way, and 14 signs or clear words. Each change has a reason in
  `v4/changes_f26.tsv`. In 23 more units the mark cannot tell list A from list C or from a plain name, and the

- One spelled word that version 3 called damaged is now read: HA GIUSTIFICATO (line 17).
- All proposals are in `v4/interp_v4.tsv`: 16 are kept from version 3 and 31 are new (both letters). This letter
  uses 32 of them in 41 units.

## Signs in the text

CAPITALS = spelled with the symbol alphabet. Plain = value with a period gloss. {braces} = window proposal.
*Italic* = clear text in the letter. ⟨angle⟩ = supplied by me. [..] = not resolved; a number inside is the figure.
(?) = doubtful. The code gives dictionary forms: number, gender and tense are mine.
[N28] = the figure 28 with a bar above (a title or a person). [O] = a tall oval sign (a name).

## Persons

- [N28] di [O]: the cipher does not name him in a way that the table can read. lists_v4 gives "Cardinale d'Este" for 28
  (a gloss of 1586); that cardinal died in December 1586, so the value is not used. The history check (below) says:
  the Duke of Luxembourg-Piney.
- "Cardinale di Borbone": the plain figure 23. This is now a glossed value (fr. 4696 f. 122v). In version 3 it was an
  inference from history.
- "i due cardinali Spagnoli": the plain figure 60 = "Spagnoli" (fr. 4696 f. 44v).

## History check

See `v4/NOTES.md`, section 5. In short: Pastor (History of the Popes, vol. 21, pp. 350-358) confirms the departure
for Loreto on 7 March, the report that the Pope would yield to Spain, the audience in which Olivares asked pardon,
the congregation of 19 March (23 cardinals called, 3 absent: 20 present), its vote, the demand to free the
Cardinal of Bourbon first, the Wednesday consistory of 21 March and the congregation of the next morning, the
letters that were read there, the proposal to expel Olivares, the Spanish troops at the border of the Papal States,
the mission of two Spanish cardinals (Deza and Mendoza) and the delay that Olivares gave.

## How sure

- The frame rests on glossed values, spelled words and clear words. With the outside control I hold it as sure.
- A word in braces is a proposal. In the blind test of the earlier work two proposals in three were right; of nine
  proposals of version 3 that fr. 4696 later met, eight were right.
- Three words were shaped by what I knew of the events before I read Pastor: {cacciare}, scomunicava (the figure 28
  against 29) and {publica}. For {cacciare} and scomunicava the window and the shape of the digit carry the reading;
  {publica} is weak.
- Not understood: the end of line 9, the first half of line 17, the sentence on the answer from France (f. 27r
  line 4), and the last line.
- Passes: lines 6-21 and f. 27r 1-8 had two blind passes (95 % same figure, 90 % same mark) and my look, which is
  not blind. Lines 1-5 had the two blind passes only.
