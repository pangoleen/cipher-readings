## Text

You may copy, share and adapt the text for any purpose, if you give credit. A suitable credit line is:

> Paolo Rosson with Claude (Anthropic), "Readings of historical ciphers", 2026,
> https://github.com/pangoleen/cipher-readings (add the name of the folder)

The letters and their period decipherments are in the public domain. The licence covers our
transcription, reading, translation and commentary.

## Images

Most images are reduced copies of pages that the Bibliothèque nationale de France publishes on Gallica. Two are pages of printed books of 1725 and 1742, which are in the public domain. Some carry
our annotations. The source is "gallica.bnf.fr / BnF". The BnF's conditions of reuse for Gallica content apply to
them, and every reuse must keep this credit. Read the current conditions on the Gallica site
(https://gallica.bnf.fr/edit/und/conditions-dutilisation-des-contenus-de-gallica) before you reuse an image.
