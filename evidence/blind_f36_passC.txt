== NOTES ON DOUBTFUL UNITS ==
General: bars (above, below, both) and dots above are clear on this film. The weak points are
(1) dots below, because show-through from the other side makes small grey spots, and
(2) arc against circumflex, when the sign is small.
I counted the dot of a digit 1 as the digit's own dot, not as a mark.

RECTO
L01 44~^._.  FIGURE sure (two open 4). MARK in doubt: the arc and the dot above are clear. The dot below is a
             short dark stroke under the first 4; it can be the foot of the 4. Candidates: 44~^._. or 44~^.
L01 32^-     the bar above is thin and a little curved; I read a bar.
L01 80^.     MARK in doubt: a very faint dot above the 8. Candidates: 80^. or bare 80. The 8 is faint but sure.
