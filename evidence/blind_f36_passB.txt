Notes on the clear text of f. 36r:
- Three cipher figures stand in the clear text, each with a clear gloss written above it by another hand:
  78 = "D. di Mant.a" (line 03); (45) = "S. A." (line 04); the figure at the end of line 04 and again in line 07 looks
  like "59" or "5g" with a gloss that I read "55 sup.ne"(?). I am not sure of this last figure or of its gloss.
- Line 01-02: the date of the Duke's letter is "di 28 di ..."; the month is abbreviated and hard to read. The letters
  look like "Feb.o"; "7b.re" is the other possible reading. The "28" has a stroke above it.
- There is no date line on f. 36r.

Notes on the clear text of f. 36v:
- A short dash stands in the left margin before "Questo è certo" (cipher L03).
- The same small sign (like "&" with a cross-stroke) stands in lines 06, 07, 10 and 12. In line 06 it fits "già".
  I did not resolve it in the other lines.
- DATE: there is NO date line on f. 36v. The written block ends about two thirds down the page, in the middle of a
  sentence, with no closing formula and no signature. The letter must continue on the next page (f. 37r). I did not
  look for other images.
- Context that helps to date the letter: it answers a letter of the Duke "di 28 di Feb.o(?)" received two days
  before; the same day a courier of the Legate Caetano brought the news of the defeat ("rotta") given by the King of
  Navarre to the Duke of Mayenne ("Duca d'Umena"); the Pope called a general Congregation "hieri mattina".
