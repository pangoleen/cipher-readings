## What the letter says (only as far as the words carry)

1. Eighteen days before, a person (N28-di-[O]) left Rome "per una sua {devotione}". At once the report rose that the
   Pope had broken(?) with the King of Spain, who had asked several things of him "con molta {instanza}".
2. The Spanish ambassador pressed the Pope, and a formal act of the ambassador was feared "ogni {giorno} et {quasi}
   ogni hora". The Pope called a meeting of cardinals and told them what he was threatened with.
3. "il parere di {quasi} tutti fu che non conveniva a dignità di Papa il far alcuna delle cose domandate se non con
   debito {termine}", and only after the answer of Navarre to the Pope's demand to free [23] "innanzi a ogni altra
   cosa". They held that the ambassador's act had no just ground and must not be accepted.
4. The ambassador was not quieted. A second meeting followed. Two cardinals were to work, as private persons and not
   as ministers of the Pope, to draw the ambassador back from "questa impresa". They did so, and "siano usciti ... per
   qualche {giorno} di uno grande {travaglio} et pericolo".
5. The key point is the freeing of [23], to which the Cardinal does not know how Navarre will consent.
6. "Papa più di ⟨una volta⟩ HA promesso(?) a Re di Spagna di non havere mano in fatto o vero consentire alcuno per Re
   di Francia se non È ... Catolico, ma grato et confidente a ⟨S.M.tà⟩."
7. In clear: he complains of the labour of this cipher ("penando tanto a scriver con questa cifra").

## Inference from outside the text (not read in the cipher)

The events fit the known crisis of March 1590 between Sixtus V and the Spanish ambassador Olivares over the envoy of
the Catholic royalists, the Duke of Luxembourg-Piney, who went to Loreto and came back; [23] would then be the old
Cardinal of Bourbon, Navarre's prisoner. These two names are my identification from history. The cipher gives only
"N28-di-[O]" and the plain figure 23. The clerk of fr. 4702 read a round sign as "Campagna" on f. 97r; I cannot say
if the tall oval here is the same sign.
