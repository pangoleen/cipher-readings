# BnF Espagnol 318 no. 94: Juan de Lanuza, viceroy of Sicily, to King Ferdinand, Messina, 27 April 1503

Reading of 4 October 2026. The key is rebuilt from the letter itself (`key.py`, `KEY_TABLE.md`).
The line-by-line machine decode of both transcription passes is in `DECODE_RAW.md`. This file is the edited reading.

## Conventions

- Every cipher line had two blind transcription passes (f. 120r: one by the solver, one by a blind agent; ff. 120v and 121r: two blind agents).
- *Italic* = clear Spanish written on the page. Plain = deciphered.
- `(?)` = doubtful word. `[xxx]` = code word with no value. `...` = stretch not resolved.
- Word division, capitals and punctuation are mine. I keep the spelling of the cipher (*screvia*, *conpanya*, *senyaladamente*, *danyo*).
- The letter f: the transcription did not separate the f sign (a p with a long tail) from the a sign (a short p with a ring). I restored f by context in: *fortaleza, franceses, infantes, fuego, desfecha, Ferando, fazer, fallan, sufrir, conformes, informar*. I checked the sign on the image for *fortaleza* (f. 120r l. 7) and *franceses* (l. 19).

## English summary of what is read

Items 1, 2, 5 and 7 rest on words read letter by letter. Items 3, 4 and 6 lean on code words with no value; read them as probable, not certain.

1. **After the rout.** The viceroy wrote right after the "desbarate" (the French defeat near Gioia, 21 April 1503). He sent the *conservador* to make the captains understand the King's interest. The galley could not cross the Faro; don Fernando de Andrada, Benavides and Carvajal came and went back.
2. **The sack of the fortress of Gioia.** The place gave itself up "a merced". Don Juan de Cardona, Antonio de Leyva and Carvajal were put in the fortress so that an inventory could be made and a good account given. At nightfall the first men went in before the inventory; they found the chamber of "mosse de Aubeni" (d'Aubigny) empty. After midnight foot soldiers of don Hugo's company came in on the pretext of speaking to his brother don Juan; more and more came, they came to knife blows among themselves, and so the fortress was sacked.
3. **The treasury officers.** The contador Alonso Guerrero and the conservador reached Gioia after the army had left for the Rocca d'Angitola. The conservador required all the captains to keep the goods and the prisoners' property. The viceroy will report what he has done.
4. **Suspicion and inquiry.** The chief men of don Hugo's company are suspected of profiting from the sack. The viceroy proposes an inquiry under oath.
5. **Prisoners.** Andrada sent three chief prisoners to Messina, among them the brother of "mosse de Grani" (Grigny). They are lodged in the castle and well treated until the King orders what to do.
6. **The command.** Andrada wanted to give up the command that Portocarrero had left him. The viceroy wrote to the knights and captains that it was no honour to leave it, since he would soon join "el duque don Gonçalo" (the Great Captain).
7. **The disobedience.** The viceroy names don Hugo (de Cardona): in Portocarrero's life he already resented to serve under anyone; "those of Cardona have presumption to spare". The disorder of the foot soldiers began there. He names Benavides among those who obey, and an election in which "el conde Joan de Vintemilla" (Giovanni Ventimiglia) is named. Some knights told him they came near to stabbing don Hugo. His presumption blinds him so that nothing can be trusted to him. Ferando de Valencia goes to court; the King can ask him.
8. Date in clear: Messina, 27 April 1503.

## What is not resolved

- 64 code words have no value (107 of 677 code tokens); 6 values are guesses (grade C). They sit mostly in the stretches marked `...`.
- The f sign and the a sign are one token in the transcription (see Conventions).
- Three look-alike signs (`&`, `Z8`, `R`) were merged or confused by the passes; `&`/`Z8` = i, `R` = o. A third pass on those places can change single words.
- The clear Spanish of f. 120v ll. 12-18 and 32-34 is in a fast cursive; the two passes differ there and I did not read it myself on the image.
- The opening code word `rag`, the closing formula (`mei`, `qop`) and the nulls `H qo ... tz` at paragraph ends are not explained.
