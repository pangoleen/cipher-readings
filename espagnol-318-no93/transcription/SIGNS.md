# Sign names for BnF Espagnol 318 no. 93 (f. 118r-v)

Write one token for each sign. Put one space between tokens. Read left to right. One text line = one output line.
Each line image shows the SAME text line as three overlapping tiles (left, middle, right part), one under the other.
The tiles overlap: the last signs of one tile are the first signs of the next tile. Write them once.
Parts of the line above and of the line below show at the top and bottom edge of a tile. Ignore them.
Pale mirror writing in the background is ink from the other side of the leaf. Ignore it.

The text mixes two things:

1. CIPHER SIGNS: single invented shapes, each written apart. Use the names in the table.
2. CODE WORDS: two to four ordinary small letters written joined as one word (examples: pie, roc, roe, heg, hoe,
   hug, hif, mat, mae, gad, gab, gie, gue, pet, met, muy, myl, luq, ren, rup, nan, pif). Write them in plain
   lower-case letters as you see them.
   - The final letter with a straight descender is "q" (luq, maq). The final letter with a looped tail is "g" (heg, hug).
   - A "d" whose ascender swings far back to the left in a big arc is "d" (gad, mud). An upright "b" is "b" (gab, fab).
   - Minims are hard: write u for two joined minims, n only if it is clearly an n, m for three.
   - Some code words have a short curved stroke over the last letter (a macron). Write "~" after the word:
     me~ (looks like "mp" or "me" with a stroke over it), gie~, rie~.
   - Two special groups, very common. Write them as ONE token each:
     `gto` = a "g" joined to a "t" joined to a small "o".  `gt` = the same without the "o".
     `otto` = a small "o", two tall strokes with a double crossbar, a small "o" (looks like "o#o").
     `tto` = two short strokes with a crossbar, then a small "o" (looks like "#o"), with no "o" before it.

If you are not sure of a token, add a question mark right after it (for example `fs?`). If you cannot read it, write `??`.
A sign that is struck out or boxed: write `[del]`.
Clear Spanish (only at the end of the last line): put it inside angle brackets.

## Cipher signs

| name | shape |
|---|---|
| + | an upright cross, alone |
| ÷ | a horizontal stroke with a dot (or two dots) above and a dot below |
| Z | a large "Z" or "3" with a flat top; its last stroke runs down below the line. Very common |
| ^ | a small caret or roof ("ʌ"), like a small "a" with no bar, on the line |
| p | a pin: a straight stem with a small ring on top ("ϙ" or "ρ"). Very common |
| p+ | a pin with a ring on top AND a horizontal crossbar low on the stem (like the Venus sign "♀") |
| P | a letter "p" (bowl on the right of the stem) with a straight crossbar through its descender |
| pq | a letter "p" (bowl on the right of the stem) whose descender is crossed by a long LOOP (a lasso stroke) |
| qp | a letter "q" (bowl on the LEFT of the stem) whose descender is crossed by a long loop. Common |
| III | three short upright strokes with a crossbar; the bar often ends in a hook down at the right (looks like "tt7" or "##") |
| m | three joined minims (a small "m") standing as a sign of its own, not part of a code word |
| 4 | a small "4" or small crossed "f"/"t" with a short descender; it often follows `m` ("m4") or `Y` ("Y4") |
| h4 | a tall upright stroke with a "4" at its foot (looks like "l4" or "k") |
| oo | two small rings side by side, joined at the top (looks like "oo" or "ov") |
| e | an "e" or "ɛ" with a tail that curls down to the left ("ϱ"), standing alone |
| C. | a "C" with a dot inside |
| :/ | two dots one over the other, then a slanted stroke |
| .ll. | two short upright strokes between two dots |
| ir | two short strokes, the second with a small flag to the right (looks like "ır" or "ıv") |
| pi | two minims under one top bar ("π") |
| qpi | a "q" joined to a "π" under one top bar ("qπ") |
| u_ | two short minims standing on a base line (looks like "u" underlined, or "ıı" on a foot stroke) |
| n- | a small "n" whose last stroke runs on as a long horizontal bar to the right |
| u3 | two or three minims that end in a "3" or loop below the line |
| dl | a delta: round body, stem bent back to the left ("δ" or "∂") |
| 5 | a small "5" or "8" with a flat bar on top ("ƽ") |
| 7 | a "7" or angle (">" opening down-left) |
| tb | a letter "b" with a crossbar on its ascender (the bar can be long, like "T" over "b") |
| tb2 | the same "b" with TWO crossbars on its ascender |
| ff | two tall, heavy strokes with a DOUBLE crossbar through both; upright, like "#" with tall legs |
| fs | two tall slanted long-s strokes with hooked tops, light; ONE crossbar on the first stroke only (looks like "fſ" or "tſ"). Very common |
| cf | a small "c" or "ɛ" joined to one tall long-s with a crossbar (looks like "cſ" or "ct"); shorter and narrower than `fs` |
| X | a plain "x" cross |
| Xo | an "x" with a small ring or hook at the top of one arm |
| X: | an "x" with a dot above and a dot below |
| Y | a capital "Y" |
| Y= | a capital "Y" with a double line under it |
| y | a small "y" or gamma with a thick foot that turns right |
| y- | a "y" with a long flat bar on top and a hooked tail |
| psi | a cup or ring on a tall stem (looks like "Ψ", "φ" or a large open "ϙ"); bigger than `p` and open at the top |
| O. | a circle with a dot or short stroke inside ("Θ") |
| 2= | a small "2" or "z" with a double line under it |
| 2~ | a "2" or "Z" whose top is a curved hook ("Շ"), no underline |
| =8 | two horizontal bars over a small loop |
| Z= | a tall "Z" or "E"-like sign crossed by two horizontal bars, with a long tail to the lower left |
| tri | a small triangle that points down |
| tt | two slanted strokes with a crossbar, alone (no "o" after it) |
| h | a letter "h" standing alone |
| lo | a letter "l" joined to "o" |

If you see a sign that is not in the table, give it a short new name and describe it at the end of your answer.
