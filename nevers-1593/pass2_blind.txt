GENERAL NOTES
A. I saw no normal closed 8 and no normal 5 with a flat top bar. I saw no 2.
   The only possible 1 is the tall stroke in B1L1.
B. The u sign has two forms. Loop form: small closed loop at lower left, rising stroke, hook
   down at the right (like an 8 open at the top). Cup form: open cup at the left and an arch at
   the right. I wrote u for the two forms. Cup form at: B1L2 signs 1 and 41 and the ^9u^;
   B1L3 sign 44; B1L4 signs 2 and 7; B2L2 sign 4 and the first "uu" pair (signs 11-12);
   B2L3 sign 10. The list is not complete for B2L1, where the top is cut.
C. The 0 after a 6 or a 3 is frequently a cup open at the top (B1L1 17, B1L2 31, B2L3 29).
   The 0 after 7 is a closed oval.
D. Many 9 have only a very small loop or hook. A long stem separates them from 4.
E. Dots stand at mid height, not on the base line. Small dots that touch the left tip of a 4
   or the loop of a 6 can be pen starts and not separators.
F. Tails of letters from the line above and tops of signs from the line below show in many
   pictures. I ignored them.
G. Gaps. Typical blank between signs: 20 to 45 px in the enlarged picture. In B2L4 the blanks
   between the groups are 62 to 83 px, and I show them as spaces. Other blanks of 60 px or more
   (not shown as spaces): B1L1 between signs 27 and 28 (61); B1L2 between 4 and 5 (88) and
   between 8 and 9 (68); B1L3 between 6 and 7 (69); B2L2 between 2 and the dot (60) and
   between "u4" and "uu" after the strike (88); B2L3 between signs 45 and 46 (61).
