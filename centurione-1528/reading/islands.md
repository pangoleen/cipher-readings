# The stretches that read as connected sense

These are the only parts of the letters that this folder calls "read". Rule: at least 6 cipher units in a row, all with a value of class sure or probable in `key/table.tsv`, with no guess and no unknown group between them, and the stretch reads as connected sense.

- CAPITALS: words that stand in clear in the letter.
- A single letter (a, e, i, o, r, n ...) is a spelling sign (numbers 6 to 9).
- `*` or `?` marks a place that rests on a hypothesis about one sign.
- The spelling is the writer's: soa, toa, zifra, scripto, vinti, octo.
- The English gloss is ours.

To see the cipher groups under each stretch, apply the key to a transcription with `decode.py`.
