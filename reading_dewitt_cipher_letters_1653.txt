READINGS - letters in the De Witt cipher, Thurloe vol. 1 (NOT the Boreel ring cipher)

Result first: this is another cipher, and its key is in print since 1906. Nothing here is a new solution.
- Key: R. Fruin, in Brieven van Johan de Witt, i (ed. Fruin and Kernkamp, Amsterdam 1906), p. 72:
    1-8 a | 9,10 b | 11,12 c | 13,14 d | 15-19 e | 20,21 f | 22,23 g | 24,25 h | 26-30 i | 31,32 k | 33,34 l | 35,36 m
    37,38 n | 39-43 o | 44,45 p | 46,47 q | 48,49 r | 50,51 s | 52,53 t | 54-58 u,v | 59,60 w | 61,62 x | 63,64 y | 65,66 z
  (read by me in the OCR text of resources.huygens.knaw.nl/retroboeken/dewitt, source 3, page 97; not on a page image).
- Kernkamp, same volume, p. 92 note 2: the key reads the ciphered places of Beverningh to De Witt of 27 and 30 June 1653
  "(bij Thurloe I p. 301, 308, 309)", except a part of the passage on p. 309, and the letter of 18 July 1653
  "(bij Thurloe I p. 339, 340)". He does not print the readings of 27 and 30 June there.
- What I did: before I found Fruin's key I saw that the numbers are in alphabet order with 1 to 5 numbers per letter,
  and I read "representatif", "factie.. ende verdeeltheden", "desperaet", "audientie". My ranges were inside Fruin's.
  So "key rebuilt" is true, and "key already published" is also true. Fruin's table is the reference.

All groups below were checked on the page images of the 1742 print (archive.org). BHO drops groups in two places
(i.301: "5. 37. 14." missing, and the signature "H h h h" is mixed into the line; i.308: "15. 59. 60. 16." missing).
[x] = letter repaired by me; the print has another number. Misprints in the print are frequent (see NOTES.md).

1. Beverning to De Witt, London 17/27 June 1653. Thurloe i.301-302.
   "... a great accroachment about our 24. 5. 37. 14. 7. 34. 29. 38. 22. 19 ;"
   h a n d a l i n g e  ->  HAND[E]LINGE   (7 for 17)
   Printed Dutch text: none found. Kernkamp 1906 says the place can be read; he gives no reading.

2. Beverning to De Witt, 30 June 1653 N.S. Thurloe i.308-309.
   a) "to the 37. 26. 15. 59. 60. 16. 48. 15. 45. 49. 16. 50. 17. 37. 52. 4. 53. 26. 20. to be addressed"
      NIEWWE REPRESENTATIF. Thurloe's print has the gloss "new representatives" above it: read in 1653 by the English.
   b) "none have any knowledge of our negotiation 52. 60. 16. 34. 32. 19. 38. 18. 29. 37. 12. 49. 28. 50. 26. 51. 53.
       47. 17. 52. 42. 36. 52. 18. 38. 15. 19. 37. 18. 38. 15. 19. 37. 18. 38. 42. 20. 53. 16. 37. 6. 38. 14. 17. 49.
       18. 37. 29. 37. 11. 29. 49. 53. 17. 37. 57. 64. 53. 52. 19. 9. 16. 48. 50. 53. 15. 37 ;"
      t w e l k e n e i n c r i s i s t q e t o m t e n e e n e n e e n e n o f t e n a n d e r e n i n c i r t e n
      v y t t e b e r s t e n
      -> 'T WELKE NE(?) IN CRISI ST[A]ET OM TEN EENEN [EENEN] OFTE(N) ANDEREN IN C[O]RTEN UYT TE BERSTEN
      "which stands at a crisis, and will soon break out one way or the other."
      Doubts: "ne" (2 letters), 47 (q) where "staet" needs a, a group of 5 numbers printed twice, 29 (i) for o.
      This is the passage that Kernkamp could read only in part.
   Printed Dutch text: none found.

3. Beverning and Nieupoort to De Witt, 18 July 1653. Thurloe i.339-340. 207 groups.
   REPRODUCED. The Dutch letter is in print with the cipher solved: Brieven aan Johan de Witt, i (ed. Fruin and
   Japikse, 1919), pp. 80-83; before that Nedermeijer van Rosenthal, Bijdragen voor Vad. Gesch. en Oudh. x, p. 291.
   Fruin's key on Thurloe's groups (my repairs in []), against the 1919 text:
     factie[n] ende verdeeltheden          1919: "de factiën ende verdeyltheden"
     het interest [van 330]                1919: "het interest van de Prince van Oraigne"   (330 = Prince of Orange)
     [n]yet [sullen] suff[i]s[a]nt syn     1919: "niet sullen suffisant sijn"
     tot [d]e bedieninghen van syne voorouders nyet van nu af ge[d]esigneert worde
                                           1919: "tot de bedieningen van syne voorouders niet van nu aff gedesigneert worde"
     bekennen dat wy ten uyterst[e]n daerover becomm[ert] zyn
                                           1919: "bekennen, dat wy ten uyttersten daerover becommert zijn"
     hyer | desperaet | handeling | audientie | da[t] point | [p]ropositien [i]n den staet | kennisse
                                           1919: the same words
   12 of 207 groups are wrong in Thurloe's print (57, 97, 19, 9, 17, 24, 6, 6, 51, 13, 48, 70). Three are a "6" where
   the key needs 16. The 1919 text has more ciphered text after "onvermydelijck geschyeden moet"; Thurloe stops there.

4. De Witt to Beverning, [24 July 1653]. Thurloe i.351. REPRODUCED (De Witt's letters to the deputies are in Brieven
   van De Witt, i, pp. 91-258; I did not compare this letter word for word). Fruin's key on the BHO text gives:
   "princes(se) dovariere", "graef willem", "hooch(t)", "designeren", "hoff", "haere hooch m".

5. Beverning to Nieupoort at The Hague, Westminster 22 Aug 1653 N.S. Thurloe i.418. Groups checked on the page image.
   "I spoke with 66 63 37 15 6 26 16 in the Latin tongue"      z y n e a i e   -> ZYN E[X]IE (= zyn Ex.ie; 6 for 61?)
   "chiefly of 51 16 12 58 29 26 52 17 64 53"                   s e c v i i t e y t -> SECU[R]ITEYT (29 for 49)
   "whether 66 63 38 17 61 did understand"                      z y n e x       -> ZYNE EX. (His Excellency = Cromwell)
   The code groups 297, 369, 298, 373, 296, 434, 329 of this letter are not letters and stay unread.
   Printed Dutch text: none found (not in Brieven aan De Witt; the Verbael was not searched for it).
   Not in Kernkamp's list of 1906. The key is Fruin's, so this is a small application of a published key.
