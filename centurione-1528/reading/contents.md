# What the letters are about

Only stretches that read as connected sense are used here ([islands.md](islands.md)). A point marked (guess)
rests on a weaker value. The summaries are ours; a historian must check each point.

## BnF fr. 3022, no. 20: Madrid, 11 April 1528, to "Garbino" at Lucca (the writer says "voi")

- **f. 44r.** The writer wrote before by way of Genoa, "per man de Hieronimo". He writes all the time to
  Hieronimo and has ordered him always to send the addressee a careful copy. He is "senza alcun agiuto". He does
  not repeat the news, because through Hieronimo the addressee has heard everything. He speaks of a copy of
  "zifra nostro".
- **f. 44v.** "As I have always told you, and God willing, I hope to be resolved in any case" at Valencia, where
  His Majesty goes "a li vinti del presente". The clear postscript says the same.
- **f. 45r.** A lord must stay in the good grace of His Majesty (his title: guess). The Grand Chancellor is a
  friend to him (two words: guess). The writer serves "as if all my life I had eaten the bread of" that lord.
  "Keep all this to yourself, if you have no way to make it known very cautiously."
- **f. 45v.** His Majesty has provided a sum in ducats for Italy (460 thousand: guess). The armies in the
  Kingdom are thought to have fought a battle. Reports from Genoa. A plan to take "the gold" as a loan (and the
  silver of the churches: guess); "he has not yet wanted to do it".
- **f. 46r.** Who will go to Italy and govern Naples: "alcuna volta si e parlato del gran cancellero, alcuna de
  ..., alcuna del arcivescovo de Toledo"; now nobody is named, until the outcome of the armies is known. Latin
  tags: "secundum subiectam materiam". "Let it all stay with you."
- **f. 46v.** Few stretches read. Somebody "did not want", "saying that he cannot do it without" an order.

## BnF fr. 2988, first letter signed "Hieronimo Ranzo" (the writer says "tu")

- Letters written "con lacte" (with milk, as invisible ink) could mostly not be read. The son must leave more
  room between the lines written in ink.
- "On the matter of the office I confirm what I wrote." Hope for the offices in Naples. The Grand Chancellor
  "lo desidera et procura".
- The safe-conduct.
- "Advertisi sopra tuto, per quanto ti e cara la vita mia, de non comunicar questo con persona del mondo altra
  che con toa madre."
- A large packet went with "Cataneo nostro".
- Close: "... quanto ho scripto per le altra de Madrid a vinti sette de marzo mille cinquecento vinti octo".
  A postscript follows (its day: guess): another letter "in zifra" has come; "the matter of the Union".
  "Vale. Tuo padre ti saluta."

## BnF fr. 2988, second letter signed "Hieronimo Ranzo" (duplicate in fr. 3019; the writer says "tu")

- **Page 1.** "Como gia piu volte ti ho scripto": the son reports too little. News came only from Iacobo,
  above all of the things at Savona. The son must learn everything day by day. What His Majesty must see goes in
  separate letters, in the large cipher, in a disguised hand, signed with another name, "adrizandole qua a
  Hieronimo Ranzo", by way of "messer Bartolome Manno". The Emperor and "these lords of the Council" will see
  them.
- **Page 2.** Rules of safety: a second, harmless letter beside the secret one; addresses at Valencia ("Bernabe
  Grimaldo") and Cartagena. A new cipher comes with Cataneo. "Io non ti scrivero in zifra senza grande
  necessita"; at times he will use milk, and such letters "will begin Fili dilectissime, where I usually put
  Amantissime fili". Keep ciphers, letters and copies hidden. He does not want to be drawn into the matter of
  the Union now.
- **Page 3.** His own career. He asks for "una presidentia in la Camera de la Sumaria de Napoli", vacant, "for
  life". "The favour that the said Grand Chancellor does me" will overcome the many competitors.
- **Page 4.** A man left for Italy with money for bills of exchange for the army. "Let it all stay buried." The
  safe-conduct again. "Mandoti una altra adicione del zifra presente"; the son must add "in order what is
  missing". Close: "Vale. Ex Madrid a di ... de marzo mille cinquecento vinti octo" (no day number stands in the
  groups).

## What stays open

- 242 groups (280 tokens) have no value.
- The rule of the middle of the small alphabet (plain i, l, m, n, o, p).
- The sums of money and most day numbers.
- The persons: Bartolome Manno, Bernabe Grimaldo, Nicolo de ..., Polo, Iacobo. The sheet of cover names in the
  volume (fr. 3022, ff. 48-49) gives "Bernabe Grimaldo" as a cover name, so some of these are covers.
- Who the addressee of no. 20 is.
- The 84 tokens that are read through a corrected base letter need a check on the images.
