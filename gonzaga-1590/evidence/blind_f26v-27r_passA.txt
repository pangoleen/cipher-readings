## DOUBTFUL PLACES
==================================================================
General
- Digit 1 has its own dot. Where a dot stands over a figure with 1 (13, 41, 51, 18, 10) I cannot always tell the mark from the i-dot. I wrote the mark only when the dot is higher or heavier than the usual i-dot, and put "?" in the close cases.
- "=" (bar above and bar below) is clear in most places. Short bars over 7 can be the top stroke of the 7 itself (76=, 75=, 79=).
- Circumflex (^^) and caron (_v) are sharp on this film. Arc (~) is clear when it is a high bow.
- [tall 0]: a tall narrow oval, 5 times on these pages (f.26v L06, L08, L09, L11 after (24); f.27r L08). It is not one of the listed symbols. It may be a nought used as a sign.
