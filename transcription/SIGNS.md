# Sign names for BnF Espagnol 318 no. 94 (ff. 120r-121v) and no. 93 (f. 118)

Write one token for each sign. Put one space between tokens. Read left to right.
The text mixes three things:

1. CIPHER SIGNS (single invented shapes, each written apart). Use the names below.
2. CODE WORDS: two to four ordinary small letters written joined as one word, with a space on each side
   (examples: raz, muy, mas, pat, mub, nob, mal, malz, qa, qaq, lue, mys, nyl, nyb, mok, otto).
   Write them in plain lower-case letters as you see them. The letter that looks like a "3" with a tail at the end of
   a word is "z" (raz, malz). The q-shaped letter with a straight descender is "q" (qa, qaq, qik).
   Minims are hard: write u for two joined minims and n only if it is clearly an n; m for three.
3. CLEAR SPANISH: put it inside angle brackets, for example <y no poder passar>.

If you are not sure of a token, add a question mark right after it (for example `3o?`). If you cannot read it, write `??`.

## Cipher signs

| name | shape |
|---|---|
| 7 | a "7" |
| 6 | a "6" |
| 3 | a "3", often with a flat top |
| 3o | a "3" or "z" that ends in a tail with a small ring, tail goes right |
| 5 | a "5" or capital "S" shape, no ring |
| 5o | a "5"/"S" shape that ends in a stroke with a small ring to the right (looks like "So" or "5o") |
| G | a tall "5" or "G" shape whose lower stroke runs far down to the left under the line (looks like "ɧ" or a long "ʂ") |
| 8 | a small "8", sometimes open at the top, sometimes with a bar over it |
| 9 | a "9" or "g" or theta-like round sign with a curl, not a letter q |
| III | three short slanted strokes ("///") |
| N | two minims joined, with a bar over them or a foot stroke (looks like "n" or "u" or "ıı" with a line) |
| a | a small letter "a" alone |
| ao | a small "a" or "d" with a small ring over it or on top of its stem |
| p | a short stem with a small ring on top (like a small rho "ρ" or a pin) |
| P | a "p" or "ρ" whose descender has two crossbars (looks like "₱" upside, a p on a double cross) |
| k | a "k" or "R"-like tall letter |
| x | an "x" whose right stroke runs long down to the left (the commonest sign; looks like "ӿ" or "4") |
| X | a plain upright cross "×" or a large "X" without the long tail |
| + | an upright cross "+" alone |
| al | alpha shape "α" (like "oc" joined, or a fish loop) |
| dl | a "d" with a round body and the stem bent back left (like "ð" or "δ") |
| tb | a "b" with a crossbar on its stem (looks like "t" joined to "b", "ƀ") |
| tbo | the same crossed "b" with small rings at the ends of the crossbar |
| ot | a small "o" joined to a cross "+" (looks like "ot") |
| o- | a small "o" with a horizontal stroke to the right (like "σ" or "o–") |
| = | two short horizontal strokes one over the other, the lower often a dot (like "=" or "÷" without top dot) |
| :. | three or four dots in a group |
| ss | two tall long-s strokes side by side ("ſſ"), no crossbar |
| ff | two long slanted strokes with a crossbar through them, descending below the line (looks like "ff" or "4f" or "tt") |
| F | one long "f" or long-s with one or two crossbars |
| QQ | two "q" shapes side by side with a double bar through both descenders |
| Q | one "q" with a double bar through its descender |
| q | a "q"/"9" shape standing alone as a sign (not in a code word) |
| & | a large "8"-like loop with a long tail that swings down to the left (like "&" or "Ȣ") |
| Z8 | an "8"-like loop standing on a "Z" or "L"-like base stroke |
| R | a small loop with a tail that crosses down to the right (like "ɤ", "℞" or a ribbon) |
| B | a large "B" with a flourish on top |
| ß | a "ß" or "β" shape with a long descender |
| C. | a "C" with a dot inside |
| M | a capital "M" |
| V | a "V" or "ν" with a loop on its left arm |
| Z | a big "Z" or "2" |
| E | a "ɛ" (reversed 3) |
| A | a capital "A" |
| l | a tall single "l"-like stroke with a small curl, standing alone |
| H | two tall strokes with a crossbar, like "tH" or "tt" or "++", standing alone (not inside "otto") |
| qo | a "q" with a small ring over it and a comma-like tail |
| tz | "t" joined to a "z"-like sign with dots around it |
| . / , | punctuation as written |

If you see a sign that is not in the table, give it a short new name and describe it at the end of your answer.
