# Notes (what I see / what I infer)
# - Seen: f. 94r lines 1-2 give the last cipher line of the letter (fr. 4698 f. 31r); the clerk wrote it first. Lines 3-16
#   decipher f. 30r; from line 16 "ha dato occasione" the text deciphers f. 30v.
# - Seen: the struck words are the clerk's first decodings. Inferred: most are values of the same figure in another list
#   (a mark read wrongly), for example "purga(tione)" then "persone", "detto" then "bene", "infedeltà" then "lettera".
# - Inferred: "inetitud.e sede" (94r 13) is a wrong decoding of the word before SEDE (sense: "questa santa sede").
#   "benche" (94r 19 and 24) stands where the sense asks for "essere". "agiotam.te" (94v 8) stands where the sense asks
#   for "ordine". "purgatione" (94r 19) stands for "persona". "intermidirsi" = INTEPIDIRSI and "sprezar" = SPERAR are
#   misreadings of spelled words. So the clerk's text is not free of errors.
