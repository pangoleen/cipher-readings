# Two period decipherments of other cipher letters of Badalocchio

Found on 11 October 2026, after our first reading of f. 80r. **They were not used to build the key.** They are
not decipherments of the three letters of this folder. This file says what they are, what they prove, and what
they do not prove. It is a report by a model, from images of Gallica. The quotes are one reading.

"None found" has limits:

- **BnF fr. 4718, ff. 6 to 11 (nos. 3-5), and fr. 4701, f. 63 bis (no. 17), are not online. We did not see them.**
  The printed catalogue describes fr. 4718 nos. 3-5 as cipher letters of 1592, one of them signed by Badalocchio
  and dated 12 December 1592. It describes fr. 4701 no. 17 as a decipherment of an unsigned letter of 1592 to
  Lyon. A period decipherment of one of our letters can exist there, or on a leaf that the catalogue does not
  name.
- We did not page through fr. 3982 and fr. 3983. We went to the numbers of the catalogue.

## The two decipherments

Both are three pages of clear Italian, with no figures and no signature, in one hand: the round hand of a clerk.
It is not Badalocchio's hand. The headings and the notes on the back are in a rapid French hand ("11 de Dec.
1592"; "du 6 feb. 1593 Badalochio"). The Italian has French spellings: "que", "luy", "comme", and "chi" for "che".

**BnF fr. 3982 no. 96, ff. 194r-195r (Gallica `btv1b9060543f`, views 397-399): 11 December 1592, 63 lines.**
Advice for Navarre, which the Duke must pass on. He is called king for the fourth year and stays a heretic. He must
make a full profession of faith and write to the Pope and to the cardinals. He must send a secret and able person
to Rome.

- "Volge il quarto anno che Navara fu chiamato re di francia"
- "nientedimeno / persevera heretico"
- "la regina d'Inghilterra et gli protestanti"
- "per salvar l'anima sua"; "per mettere il regno suo in pace et quiete"

**BnF fr. 3983 no. 35, ff. 74r-75r (Gallica `btv1b9059406b`, views 134-136): 6 February 1593, 74 lines.** The
audience of Badalocchio with the Pope on Sunday 31 January 1593. The Pope will hear nobody for Navarre. He tells
Badalocchio to write that the Duke must leave Navarre.

- "ne il / Card.le de Gondi ne il Marchese di Pisani"
- "me rispose che lasci - / Navara"
- "quello ch'altra volta gli ho detto"

A note on the shelfmarks: one copy of the catalogue gives these pieces at ff. 196-198 and ff. 76-77. The numbers
on the pages are 194, 195 and 74, 75.

## What they prove, and what they do not

- **A direct test of the key: none.** The cipher originals of these two letters are not in fr. 4700. We think that
  the original of 11 December is fr. 4718 no. 3. We did not see it.
- **They support the sense of our readings.** The same writer treats the same matter in the same words: "il quarto
  anno", "persevera", "l'anima", the demand that the Duke leave Navarre, and his answer about the wrongs done to
  him. [audit.md](audit.md), part 5.1, has the table.
- **They do not test single groups.**
- **What would make them a full test:** images of fr. 4718 ff. 6 to 11. Then fr. 4718 no. 3 with fr. 3982
  ff. 194r-195r is a known plaintext of 63 lines for this cipher.
