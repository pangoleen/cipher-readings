# What the letters say

Only the islands of `islands.md` are used. A point marked **(guess)** rests on a value of class guess or on an
inference; it is not "read". The summaries are ours. A historian must check each point. 

Document numbers in the tables below: D01 to D06 are fr. 3022 ff. 44r, 44v, 45r, 45v, 46r, 46v (letter no. 20).
D07 and D08 are the two pages of the first "Ranzo" letter (fr. 2988, Gallica views 6 and 7). D09 to D12 are the
four pages of the second "Ranzo" letter (views 17 to 20).

## BnF fr. 3022, no. 20: Madrid, 11 April 1528 (the writer says "voi")

**f. 44r.**
- The writer wrote before by way of Genoa, "per man de Hieronimo". He has now four letters of the addressee.
- New: "ho supli-to con quello che ho scripto de continuo a Hieronimo". He made up for the missing answer with his
  letters to Hieronimo. Hieronimo has the order to send a copy each time.
- New: he is "senza alcun agiuto" and has "ocupacione che qua se mi dano continuamente". So he "served both with
  one writing" ("ho suplito ad ambi con uno scrivere").
- Hieronimo "anche mi adviso haver vi mandato copia del zifra nostro, del qual vi potrete valere".

**f. 44v.**
- "Se havero da venir in Italia con ..." (two groups open).
- New, one sentence now: "spero esser ad ogni modo resoluto arrivando in Valenza, per dove soa maesta partira a li
  vinti del presente". The clear postscript says the same.

**f. 45r.** This page was 29 % read. It is now 81 %.
- The addressee has reported about "the lord Duke" ("che havete denotato del signor duca"). The reports were
  welcome to the Grand Chancellor.
- New: "Desidera grandemente soa signoria che soa excellentia ... conservi in bona gratia de soa maesta, si como
  la e al presente, in modo che soa maesta habia a certificarsi esser lo animo suo inclinato a soa solita
  devotione et servitio de soa maesta, et che le contrarie opere soe fatte non siano voluntarie salvo violente."
  The Grand Chancellor wants the Duke to stay in the Emperor's grace. The Emperor must be sure that the Duke's
  acts against him were forced.
- New: this is "the true and ... way to bring him into port" ("la vera et [..] via a condurlo a porto").
- New: the Duke "will have seen a good sign" when the Emperor did something "a li ambassatori de la liga" (the
  verb is open).
- New: "ho advisato soa excellentia, per [via] de Hieronimo, de ordine del cavalier Bilia suo ambassator, el
  qual ... in compagnia de li altri ambassatori liganti". The Duke's ambassador is "the knight Bi-li-a".
  **(The word "cavalier" is probable but stands against the order of the list. The name is spelled bi-li-a.)**
- "Il signor gran cancellero li e in vero bon amico et servitor; ne io manco di esserlo ... como se tuta mia vita
  havesse mangiato el pane de soa excellentia." The Duke's orator can bear witness (the word "testimonio" is a
  guess).
- "Tuto questo reste in voi, se non haveti modo de farlo intendere cautissimamente a soa excellentia solo."
- **Who is the Duke? (inference, not read.)** A cavalier Bilia was the envoy of Francesco Sforza, Duke of Milan.
  The second Ranzo letter says that after the death of Bourbon "the Duke" must stay in "that state". The name
  of the state there is a guess (m210 "milano", against the order). So the Duke of Milan is probable, not sure.
  The title "excellentia" (s273) is inferred from "duca".

**f. 45v.**
- "Soa maesta ha proveduto in Italia de ... millia ducati et pagara altri ...". **The numbers are guesses**
  (q37, s229, s231, c191). A man "ha platicato novamente" a part of the sum; his name **(guess: Ansaldo)** and
  the place **(guess: Milano)** are open.
- New: he "waits to know what has happened with the armies in the Kingdom, and thinks that they have fought a
  battle, and hopes well of it, given the reports from Genoa".
- New, one long sentence: somebody reminded the Emperor "de valersi del oro et argenti superflui de le giesie per
  alcuni anni in presto, dicendo esser cosa a niun preiudiciale, et che ne cambiria di subito una infinita,
  essendo richissime. Ma no lo ha anche voluto fare". The gold and the surplus silver of the churches, as a loan.
- New: "Pur se la guerra perseverara, credo metera man ad ogni cosa, perche e deliberato giucarli del resto."
  If the war goes on, he will stake everything.

**f. 46r.**
- New: "non obstante quante ragione se li possano alegare et quante se possiano imaginare", they "do not want
  to hear of it". "Bisogna lassar quelle consideracione da canto."
- New: the worst thing at court "e la inresolucione, che mai o tanto tardi si resolveno ... che ogni effecto
  lor e le piu volte fora di tempo". "If God had not helped them ... stariano freschi."
- They have not yet decided on the persons to send to Italy, "como per il governo del regno de Napoli. Alcuna
  volta si e parlato del gran cancellero, et alcuna de [..], et alcuna del arcivescovo de Toledo, et de altri".
  Now nobody is named; they wait for the outcome of the armies. The second name is still open.
- New: "No vedendo che [..] basti a remediare tali inconvenienti, resto talmente atonit[o] che ogni calculo
  perdo."
- "Non vi haverete piu a fundare in li soliti dessigni, salvo guidarli de qui avanti secundum su[b]iectam
  materiam." Then: "poi mi alargo con voi confidentemente de simile cose". Latin: "ponere os in celum".

**f. 46v.** This page was 20 % read. It is now 66 %.
- New: "Qua si cognosce optimamente quanto convien a soa maesta la amicitia con Venetiani, et ne hano qua molto
  tentato in secreto lo orator suo; ma lui non ha voluto prestare o-re-[chi]-e, dicendo non poterlo fare senza
  ordine de la Signoria." The court sounded the Venetian orator in secret. He refused to listen without an order.
- "Ma mi dubito che li desfidi poi seguiti ..." (the challenges of January 1528).
- New: "Ho per certo che haveriano da soa maesta tute le honeste condicione che sapessino domandare, et senza
  alcun dubio de inobservantia, perche oltra che soa maesta e observatore di fe ...".
- New: "... che qualsivoglia utile discordia. Lo ambassator Sanchez, che se deve gia esser [..] da Venetia".
  **(Sanchez: probable, one place.)**
- The last three lines do not read.

## First letter signed "Hieronimo Ranzo" (D07, D08): the father to his son

- "Per le altre scripte hogi ho fatta resposta a le toe." One more letter came by way of Lyon, with lines in
  milk. **The day of February is a guess.**
- New, the whole instruction: the son did not write "con la penna ... piena de lacte" and did not let it "ben
  incorporato nel paper". He must make the ink lines "piu larg[he] luna da laltra".
- A "matter of [..]" that the father can hardly believe **(guess: Monaco)**. "Cercarai bonamente de saperne piu
  oltra."
- "Del fato de l officio affermo quanto ho scripto." He hopes for the offices "denotati in Napoli".
- New: "Non difido che, se la morte del secretario Pe-re-z in Roma ... sara stata vera, forsi soa maesta se
  servira di [me] in esso loco et che subito me li mandera, per starli in soi negocii con bon tractamento, et con
  godere etiam del utile de li detti offici in essa absentia." The father hopes for the post of the secretary
  Perez in Rome, if Perez is dead. "Questi signori ne hano parlato et il gran cancellero lo desidera et procura."
- New: this would be the best thing "per exaltacione de Agostino nostro". He hopes for a decision "avanti
  pasqua".
- New: he thinks of a pretext "to show that I leave here discontented". He wants the safe-conduct.
- "Advertisi sopra tuto, per quanto ti e cara la vita mia, de non comunicar questo con persona del mondo altra
  che con toa madre, che gia vedi quanto importa il secreto." New: "Dio elega de tuto il meglio."
- A large packet went with "Cataneo nostro". New: "persevera in scrivere continuamente tute le occorrentie".
- New: "le littere che ti scrivero per terra adrizaro ad A-da-no Centurione; sarano breve, como lui ha
  recordato".
- Date: "de Madrid a vinti sette de marzo mille cinquecento vinti octo".
- Postscript, new in one piece: "Sono a di trenta un. Dapoi ho recevuto una altra toa in zifra, data a li quatro
  del presente." The father has seen what the son "discussed with messer Simon circa el fato de la Unione". He
  will write "piu amplamente". "Iterum vale. Tuo padre ti saluta."

## Second letter signed "Hieronimo Ranzo" (D09-D12): the father to his son

**Page 1.**
- The son reports too little. Only a letter of Iacobo **(its day: guess)** told of "le cose seguite in Saona".
- New: he must note everything "nel medesimo instante".
- What the Emperor must see goes "per littere a parte, in cancellaresco" (chancery hand: new), in the large
  cipher, signed with another name, "adrizandole qua a Hieronimo Ranzo, con littera contrafatta".
- New: "le tale littere haverano da esser viste dal imperator et da tuti questi signori del consiglio". So he
  must write them "ben ornate".
- Letters go "in [mia] absentia a messer Bartolome Manno". New: "io [a] niun havero da lassare copia del zifra".

**Page 2.**
- New: the danger is that letters are taken "in palazo" (the Palace: probable, two places), "a man de
  governanti".
- New: a feigned letter beside the secret one. Addresses: "in Barzelona a ...", "in Valenza a Bernabe Grimaldo",
  "in Cartagenia a Nicolo de Fornari et compagnia".
- A new cipher comes with Cataneo; the son may share its use with Iacobo. New: "piu presto et meglio te
  adestrarai in tuto".
- "Io non ti scrivero in zifra senza grande necessita et modo cauto." Milk letters "comenzarano Fili
  dilectissime, si como soglio fare Amantissime fili".
- New: the papers must be hidden so that "quando le toe scripture fusseno cercate, non siano mai esse trovate".
  Then the son can "con bona audacia et fermeza negare il tuto".
- New: he does not want to be drawn into the matter of the Union "da la comunita".

**Page 3.**
- New: he prefers lasting offices to "cose momentanee et per comune ingrato, del che, oltra el vulgar proverbio,
  ne sono gia, como sai ben, scarmentato". The Commune is ungrateful, and he has learned it.
- New: if they want to give him "alcuna cura", the son must say that the father "must without doubt have left
  to cross back", so that he "con honesta ne posia esser excusato".
- New: one office "is no longer to be counted on", because after "la morte de Bo-r-bone" the Emperor knows that
  "in ogni evento resta in quello stato el duca" and that he cannot keep it "volendo quietare Italia". The
  office is in "the state of ..." **(guess: Milano)**.
- New: he is "consigliato et persuaso" by friends not to "lose himself after vain hopes".
- "Procura soa signoria de far mi provedere de una presidentia in la camera de la sumaria de Napoli, che ha
  vacato", and "the office that the late secretary B-ri-se-g-no had" there ("quondam" is a guess).
- New: one difficulty: the man before him "was a doctor" ("esser stato dotore").
- New: "abenche li sia chi per esso solo offer[e] quatro millia ducati".

**Page 4.**
- New: the man who left for Italy with money is "To-ma-so de Fornari". He carries "suma de denari per pagamento
  de cambii" for the army. He wants "the office of receiver in the said army".
- New: "lo hano remeso a li ... con littere de recomendacione vulgare de soa maesta"; "lui si e pasciuto de
  questo fumo". "Ma il tuto resti sepulto."
- "Di me sempre dirai che ho da repassare." The safe-conduct again.
- "Mandoti una altra adicione del zifra presente." New: the son must go through it ("la recorrerai") and then
  send it on to a person whose name is open.
- Date: "ex Madrid a di ... de marzo mille cinquecento vinti octo". No day stands in the groups.
