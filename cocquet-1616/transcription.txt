THE 10 DIFFERENCES
L01 last sign       pass1 t   pass2 T      -> t (value e, "maieste"); it is no taller than the "ot" before it. Doubtful by shape.
L02 sign 4          pass1 ub  pass2 nb/ub  -> ub (key: I, row 3; "auoit").
L02 "z ? mu 6"      pass1 ep  pass2 8/ep   -> ep ("croy"); an 8 gives no value in the key alphabet.
L03 "? g"           pass1 xi  pass2 ep     -> xi ("ne"); by shape it is doubtful, "re" gives no sense.
L03 "th ? t"        pass1 pi  pass2 PI     -> neither: it is the second doubling character of the key (two stems, two bars), named DBL.
L05 first sign      pass1 o   pass2 del    -> del (a small delta with a short stem; "a don").
L09 last sign       pass1 th  pass2 ob     -> th/ob cannot be told apart by shape (see NOTES); value s ("pais").
L10 sign 1          pass1 f   pass2 sl/f   -> f ("aneantir"); the foot stands on the line, as in f.
L10 sign 2          pass1 S   pass2 s/S    -> same sign as s (value n), written larger.
L11 "6 ?"           pass1 th  pass2 ob/th  -> as L09 (value s, "pays").
