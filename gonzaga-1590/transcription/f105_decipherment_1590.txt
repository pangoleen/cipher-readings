Period decipherment (Nevers's office hand) of the cipher lines of f. 17r-v
(Scipione Gonzaga to Nevers, Rome, 16 Feb 1590).  It is NOT the plaintext of f. 104r.

NOTATION for the cipher (used in all files)
  NN        plain two-digit figure            (NN)   figure in round brackets
  NN-       bar above        NN_  bar below          NN=    bar above and below
  NNd       one dot above    NNdd two dots above
  NNb       one dot below    NNbb two dots below
  NN^       circumflex above NNv  caron (check) below
  NN~       arc above = VERB SIGN (Scipione's rule, f. 17v)
  Symbols of the letter alphabet are written with look-alike characters:
  K  <>(diamond with stem = a)  V(inverted triangle = b)  /\ (lambda without bar)  T  = ‖ y R(reversed R) phi ...
  <.> = diamond with a dot in it = "et".      |  word-by-word alignment: cipher(gloss)
