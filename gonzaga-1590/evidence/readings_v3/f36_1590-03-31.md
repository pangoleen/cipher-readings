- "{secondo}": the bracket figure (12), three times, each time after "primo".

## What the letter says (only as far as the words carry)

1. After the news of Mayenne's defeat, the Pope put two questions to the Congregation: whether to do something to
   the {Legate} (I propose: recall him), and what to do "per questo accidente successo".
2. On the first, all judged "di non". On the second, "la {maggiore} parte FU di parere" that help should be given,
   "di danaro primo et poi di arme", with the King of Spain.
3. "il {secondo} FU RIMESSO a una particolare [..] di cardinali, la quale per giudicio mio non ne FARÀ niente,
   {massime} di danaro, perché tale si vede ... la inclinatione di S.S.tà."
4. f. 36v: a force sent from Rome into France is spoken of, "ma ancora di questo io ne credo poco. Certo È che fino
   hora non ve n'è alcuno {principio}."
5. It is said that Navarre wrote to Rome, but does not want to be bound. Something that the {Legate} did shortly
   before Mayenne's rout is under discussion; the Pope believes that it came from [N60] or perhaps from Mayenne.
   This part is not understood.
6. Writing to Nevers at this time is "difficilissima cosa per non dir impossibile", with the example of Volta and the
   danger to letters.

## How sure

- Point 5: weak. Point 6: clear text; the cipher part is weak.
